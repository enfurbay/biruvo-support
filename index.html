Include your app version, iPhone model and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help; hide personal information first. Never send your password, verification code, payment card details or sign-in tokens.

Using Biruvo

Start without a separate Biruvo account. Plans can be saved on your device. Sign in with Apple to sync and share through Biruvo’s Supabase service. Share one invitation link with multiple friends; they preview and accept inside the app. Shared updates are processed on the server. Keep an exported copy of important plans before changing devices. A JSON export does not include photo files.

Moving from older versions

For synchronization or invitation issues, include the message shown in Biruvo. If an older development version asked you to create a Biruvo account, contact support before removing any existing data; the current app uses local storage and Supabase. Older iCloud invitation links must be replaced by a new Biruvo invitation.

Biruvo Plus purchases

Biruvo Plus offers an annual auto-renewable subscription or a one-time, non-consumable lifetime purchase. Both unlock the same Plus features. The annual subscription renews each year unless cancelled in your App Store account settings; access continues until the paid period ends. The lifetime purchase has no automatic renewal. Apple handles payment. The price displayed in Biruvo comes from your App Store region. Use Restore purchases in the app with the Apple Account used to purchase. Biruvo links the purchase to your signed-in profile; use the same Apple sign-in when changing devices. If verification is delayed, do not buy again: reconnect and retry verification.

If you purchased a subscription, manage it in iPhone Settings under your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Removing the app does not cancel an existing subscription.

Privacy, deletion and safety reports

For an account data request, deletion request or report of abusive content, email support. Identify the relevant plan or issue only as much as needed. We may ask you to verify account ownership before handling a private-data request. Do not send someone else’s private content unnecessarily.
