Include your app version, iPhone model and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help; hide personal information first. Never send your password, verification code, payment card details or sign-in tokens.

Using Biruvo as a guest

Guest plans are saved on the device where you create them. They are not automatically shared or backed up to your Biruvo account. Use the local plan export action to keep a copy before deleting the app or changing devices.

Account and sign-in help

If your email is verified but account setup does not finish, you do not need to register again. Contact support with the message shown in the app. Account-connected services may be unavailable during development or maintenance.

Subscriptions and billing

Where Organizer Plus is available, Apple handles billing. Manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone Settings under your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting Biruvo or requesting account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription. Use Restore purchases in the app to request an entitlement check.

Privacy, deletion and safety reports

For an account data request, deletion request or report of abusive content, email support. Identify the relevant plan or issue only as much as needed. We may ask you to verify account ownership before handling a private-data request. Do not send someone else’s private content unnecessarily.
