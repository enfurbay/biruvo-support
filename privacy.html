Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Biruvo is provided by Enes Furkan Bayram under the efbapps brand. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Biruvo and these support pages. Contact: enfurbay@gmail.com.

Guest plans and local information

You can use the guest experience without creating a Biruvo account. Guest plan titles, dates, descriptions, tasks and completion state are stored locally on your device. Opening the interactive walkthrough does not create real plans, accounts or members. When you connect an Apple account, eligible plans owned only by you can migrate to that account. An original local backup is preserved. Earlier shared iCloud plans require renewed invitations; the app does not infer other people’s Supabase identities. Exporting a plan shares it through the destination you choose. Deleting the app may remove local data; device backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

Account and shared-plan information

When you sign in with Apple, we process the account identifier, email address and display name needed to sign you in and identify you to your plan members. Sign in with Apple may supply a private relay address. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth and Apple.

Shared features process information you provide, including plan and task details, dates, place labels, optional images, checklist items, memberships, assignment requests, accepted ownership and activity records. This information is used to operate your plans and show their state to authorized plan members. Do not add sensitive information that you do not want those members to see.

Notifications and technical information

Where notifications are enabled, we process device push tokens and your notification settings to deliver relevant updates. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings and use available in-app preferences. Supabase and Apple services may process technical identifiers, network information and service logs needed for delivery, security and reliability. We do not use advertising tracking or sell personal information.

Purchases

Biruvo Plus is a one-time lifetime purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive your full card details. Transaction identifiers, purchase status and an association with your Biruvo account are used to verify access, restore purchases and prevent misuse. A cancelled subscription may remain active until its paid period ends. Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions.

Support and reports

If you contact us or report content, we process your contact details, message and any information you choose to send so that we can respond and investigate. Support email is handled through Gmail. Please do not send passwords or verification codes.

Service providers and international processing

We use Supabase for authentication, shared plan data, private image storage and server notification processing, and Apple for push delivery, sign-in and purchases. The current Supabase database region is Ireland. These providers may process information in countries other than yours under their applicable terms and safeguards. Memory notes and photos are private to your signed-in account. Shared plan images and content are accessible to authorized members. Invitation links are reusable bearer links that expire after seven days or earlier if revoked.

These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure hosting. These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms or nonessential cookies added by efbapps.

Retention and deletion

Local guest content remains until you remove it or the device/app storage is removed. We retain cloud account and plan information as needed to provide the service, and support correspondence as needed to handle your request. Some transaction, security, abuse-prevention or legally required records may be retained after account deletion. Service-provider backups and retention procedures can delay final removal. Copies already exported or retained by other users are outside our direct control.

You can request access, correction or deletion by contacting support. Use the in-app account deletion action when available; if it fails or you cannot sign in, email us to request help. We verify ownership before disclosing or deleting private account information. Rights such as access, correction, deletion, objection and portability depend on applicable law. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Permissions and choices

Optional photo access and notification permissions are controlled through iOS. Biruvo does not require access to your contacts for its core planning experience. You choose whether to create an account, upload an image or export information. Turning off notifications does not delete your plans.

Children

Biruvo is designed for general social planning, not specifically for children. Do not create an account if you are below the minimum age required by applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information improperly.

Security and changes

We use platform storage protections and access controls appropriate to the service. No system can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy as features or requirements change; the effective date above identifies the current version. Significant changes will be communicated where required.
